# How to install the Knowledge-Sharing Catchup skill

This skill runs a private knowledge-sharing series for you, in your
own field, starting empty with your own questions, people, and
meetings. Nothing is shared with the person who sent it to you.

## What you need

- The skill file: `knowledge-sharing-catchup-skill.md`
- Muse (the app or website where you chat with Muse)

There is no one-tap "install agent" button. You install it by giving
the file to your Muse in chat and asking Muse to save and use it.

## Steps

1. **Get the skill file**
   Save the file sent to you: `knowledge-sharing-catchup-skill.md`.

2. **Open a new chat with Muse**
   Start a fresh chat, so the series is built for you only.

3. **Attach the skill file and send this message**

   Attach `knowledge-sharing-catchup-skill.md` to your message,
   then send:

   > Install this as a custom skill called
   > knowledge-sharing-catchup, and use it to run my
   > knowledge-sharing series.

4. **Answer Muse's setup questions**
   Muse will ask you for:
   - Your field (for example: law, medicine, engineering, design)
   - What you want to learn from people in your field
   - How long each meeting should be (30 to 60 minutes is typical)
   - What you want to call the series (for example,
     "Knowledge-Sharing Catchup")

   Example answer for a lawyer:
   > I'm a lawyer. I want to learn how other lawyers run client
   > work and keep up with new regulation. 45 minutes. Call it
   > "Law Catchup".

   Muse will then help you write your own 10 questions for your
   field. It starts empty — no people, meetings, notes, or
   summaries from anyone else.

5. **Use your series**
   - Tell Muse who you want to meet and when. Muse creates the
     calendar meeting and asks whether to add it to the tracker.
   - Before the call, Muse gives you a call sheet: who they are,
     the time and link, the questions, and the Script — the
     professional intro and closing, personalised with their name.
   - During the call, drop rough notes in chat and Muse files each
     one under the right question.
   - About an hour after the call ends, Muse asks whether the
     meeting happened. If yes, it asks whether you have a
     transcript — from Fathom, pasted here, or uploaded in the
     tracker.
   - Once the answers are saved, Muse writes the meeting summary.
     At the end of the series, it writes one all-meetings summary.

6. **Change your questions later**
   - Just tell Muse in chat, for example:
     > Change question 4 to ask about the tools they use for
     > client work.
   - Changing the questions does not change meetings that already
     have answers.

## Troubleshooting

- **Muse asks what the file is:** tell it, "It is a skill. Save it
  as knowledge-sharing-catchup and follow it to run my series."
- **People or meetings from someone else appear:** they should
  not. Start a fresh chat, install the skill again, and give Muse
  only your own field and questions.
- **You cannot find the skill file:** ask the person who sent it to
  resend `knowledge-sharing-catchup-skill.md`.

## What is not shared

Your people, meetings, notes, answers, transcripts, and summaries
are private to you. Sending or installing this skill does not give
anyone access to your Muse account, and it does not share your
series with the person who sent you the skill.
